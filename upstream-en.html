STAGE 5

RF, Power and Compound Semiconductors

GaAs, GaN and InP outside the silicon world

GaAs and GaN chips are grouped here by material; the section below groups the same chips by where they end up.

How an RF chip is made — wafer to QFN package

Side view. Wafer level on the left, QFN package level on the right. Numbers match the steps below.

1. Substrate — InP or GaAs wafer grown as the base material

2. Epi layer — grown by MOCVD on the substrate

3. Devices — RF transistors patterned into the epi layer

4. Dice — wafer cut into individual RF dies

5. Wire bond — the diced die connected to the leadframe with thin wires

6. Mould — compound poured over the die and wires

7. Leads and pad — leadframe leads and exposed thermal pad on the underside

8. Solder — leads and pad soldered flat to the PCB, no balls, no substrate

STAGE 6

Photonics and Co-Packaged Optics

Light generation, conversion and CPO

Photonic IC (PIC) plus electronic IC (EIC) form the optical engine, which sits beside the switch ASIC inside the CPO package. InP epi and substrate supply is in Stage 5.

How a CPO switch is made — InP wafer to CPO package

Side view. Laser wafer level on the left, CPO package level on the right. Numbers match the steps below.

1. Substrate — InP wafer grown as the base material

2. Epi layer — grown by MOCVD on the InP substrate

3. Laser device — laser diode structure patterned into the epi layer

4. Dice — wafer cut into individual laser dies

5. Optical engine — photonic IC (PIC) stacked on electronic IC (EIC); the laser sits with the PIC, or stays outside as an external laser source

6. Placement — optical engines placed beside the switch ASIC on one substrate; lid and coldplate over the ASIC

7. Fiber attach — FAU aligns the fiber to the PIC; fiber exits the package to an MPO connector

8. Finish — substrate mounted to the switch board on BGA balls
