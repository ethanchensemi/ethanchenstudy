What OEM Means

TermMeaningExample
OEM in Three Industries
OEM, Taiwan UsageThe factory building to the customer’s design; the same work is called EMS in the USHon Hai assembling iPhones
OEM, Servers and PCsThe brand selling finished systems under its own name, often built by ODMsDell · HPE · Lenovo · Supermicro
OEM, AutomotiveThe carmaker; its suppliers are Tier 1 and Tier 2Toyota · Tesla
Related Terms
ODMOriginal Design Manufacturer: designs and builds a product sold under the customer’s brandQuanta notebooks for HP
EMSElectronics Manufacturing Services: builds and tests to the customer’s finished designJabil · Flex · Sanmina
JDMJoint Design Manufacturer: the brand and the manufacturer split the design work–
OBMOriginal Brand Manufacturer: a former contract maker selling under its own brandASUS and Acer, which spun off Pegatron and Wistron
White BoxAn unbranded server or switch built by an ODM to the buyer’s specificationCelestica and Accton switches
ODM DirectServers sold straight from the ODM to a cloud provider, with no brand in betweenWiwynn racks for Meta
CMContract manufacturer: the umbrella term for EMS and ODM–
