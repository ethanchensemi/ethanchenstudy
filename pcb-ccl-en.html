PART A

Products

The stack, then every product by company, most advanced first, read from the chip to the board: the IC substrate under each chip, such as ABF or BT, then the CCL type and grade with the glass, copper foil and resin of its main grade, and the PCB of the board with the circuit-forming process of its type.

Overview

The Stack

Stack Labels
TermWhat It Is
Resin UncuredResin dried but only half-cured, so it flows again under heat and bonds the layers
Resin CuredResin fully hardened by heat and pressure, so it never flows again
Copper LaminatedCopper bought as foil and pressed onto the resin, then etched into circuits
Copper PlatedCopper grown in a plating bath onto drilled hole walls to join the layers, or inside a resist pattern to form fine traces
IC Substrate
TermWhat It Is
T-Glass CoreA CCL-like core of T-glass cloth and BT or low-CTE epoxy, built to stay flat under a large die
ABF Build-Up LayersResin film with no glass cloth, laminated one layer at a time on each face of the core
CCL
TermWhat It Is
Glass ClothFabric woven from glass fibre that forms the skeleton of the board
ResinThe plastic that soaks into the cloth and hardens into the glue and insulator of the board
PrepregGlass cloth soaked in resin and half-cured, which flows again under heat to bond the layers
Copper FoilReady-made copper sheet laminated onto the outside of each press
CCL CorePrepreg fully cured between copper foils, the rigid sheet every board starts from

PART B

Parameters

Dk, Df, CTE and Tg, the four numbers every IC substrate, CCL and PCB is judged on, then the M grades of CCL and the grades of glass, copper foil and resin behind them, and how the layer count of a board is written, X+Y+X.

B.1 The Four Parameters
ParameterGovernsWhat It Is
DkSpeedDielectric constant. Lower Dk lets the signal travel faster through the board
DfLossDissipation factor. Lower Df wastes less of the signal as heat. This is the number the M-series is ranked on
CTEReliabilityCoefficient of thermal expansion. No effect on signal. Decides whether the package warps or delaminates, and dominates as body size grows
TgProcess WindowGlass transition temperature. The point at which the resin softens, which caps how hot assembly can run
