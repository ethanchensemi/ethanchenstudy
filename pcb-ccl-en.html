Anatomy of the Stack

The Words the Diagram Assumes

TermWhat It Is
Glass ClothFabric woven from glass fibre. The skeleton of the board
ResinLiquid plastic that soaks into the cloth and then hardens. The glue and the insulator
Copper laminatedA ready-made copper sheet pressed onto the board. Acid removes everything that is not a circuit. The only copper bought by weight
Copper plated, through holeCopper grown onto a drilled hole wall to connect the top face to the bottom. Build-up layers are joined by laser blind vias instead
Copper plated, Semi-AdditiveCopper grown only where the circuit goes, with no foil at any point. Reaches finer lines than acid etching can

C1

Cost Structure

CCL

The share of CCL cost taken by each input and the parameters that define laminate performance.

Cost LineActualComment
Copper Foil40%Normally 35–45%. Processing fees rose 20% across the board in 3Q26, with copper metal also climbing
Glass Cloth40%Normally 25%. Rose to 40% during 2026, and is the swing factor behind CCL cost inflation
Resin20%Normally 20–30%. Smallest cost line, largest performance lever. Sets Dk, Df, CTE and Tg directly
ParameterGovernsWhat It Is
DkSpeedDielectric constant. Lower Dk lets the signal travel faster through the board
DfLossDissipation factor. Lower Df wastes less of the signal as heat. This is the number the M-series is ranked on
CTEReliabilityCoefficient of thermal expansion. No effect on signal. Decides whether the package warps or delaminates, and dominates as body size grows
TgProcess windowGlass transition temperature. The point at which the resin softens, which caps how hot assembly can run

D1

Reading the Build: X+Y+X

PCB

How a board’s build is written: X+Y+X for HDI, which adds build-up layers on each side of a core, and a plain layer count for HLC, which is all core.

ElementHow It Is MadeViasWhy It Matters
First X, build-upAdded one layer at a time. Each layer needs its own lamination, drill and plate cycleLaser blind vias, 50μm classThe expensive layers. Cost and yield risk rise faster than the layer count suggests
Y, coreA conventional multilayer stack pressed in one cycle, built from CCL Cores and PrepregMechanically drilled through-holesThe cheap layers. This is where most of the layer count sits
Last X, build-upIdentical to the first X and always equal to itLaser blind vias, 50μm classAsymmetry warps the board, so the two build-up counts are never different
