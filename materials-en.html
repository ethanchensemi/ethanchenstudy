Semiconductor Materials Map

Every material and consumable of the process map, one row each, with the steps that use it and the suppliers behind it.

How to read this page

Materials

Become part of the product, as a physical part or as the atoms of a film. Light-blue rows are what is physically left in the finished product: the die, HBM, substrate, solder, underfill, mold, lid, fibre array, optical engine. Rows marked Atoms Only supply a film’s atoms: the gas, target or bath disappears, the film stays.

Consumables

Used up making the product and gone by the end: resists and wet chemicals, slurry and pads, etch and purge gases, tapes and carriers, blades and wheels, chamber parts, probe cards and sockets.

Key light-blue row = left in the product · bold = leading supplier · green = Taiwanese supplier · after | = Chinese supplier
