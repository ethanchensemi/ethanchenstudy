E Co-Packaged Optics Optional branch
Who takes this branch

Switch silicon: Broadcom Tomahawk, NVIDIA Quantum-X and Spectrum-X, Marvell.

Who does not

GPUs, whose bandwidth bottleneck is in-package HBM, not external I/O.

TSMC COUPE stacks all three technologies at once
1SoICHybrid-bonds the 65nm electronic IC to the photonic IC to form the optical engine
2CoWoSPlaces the optical engine and the customer ASIC on one interposer
3CPOThe result: optics move inside the package, replacing the pluggable module outside it
