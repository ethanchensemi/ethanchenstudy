How to Read the Inspection Field

Next inspectionThe step is a process step, and the field lists what is verified immediately after it. Most packaging steps cannot be reworked once passed, which is why almost every one carries a check
Inspection: this step is the checkThe step is itself a test station, so there is nothing following to name. 20 steps in this document are of this kind

Inspection Methods

AOIOptical inspection for surface defects: scratches, particles, missing parts. Fast, non-destructive, 100%
MetrologyDimensional measurement: thickness, overlay, coplanarity, warpage. Fast and non-destructive
X-rayPenetrating inspection for voids, offset and cracks. Sees what AOI cannot
SAMScanning acoustic microscopy. Detects delamination and unbonded areas
SEMElectron microscopy for nanometre detail and cross-sections. Very slow, usually destructive, sampled only
STAGE 1

Wafer Fabrication

晶圓製造與晶圓級測試

"Front-end" here means position in the sequence, not a class of equipment. The purpose is to establish which die are good, because a bad die entering a stack destroys the whole package.
01

Wafer Fabrication (FEOL)

晶圓製造（FEOL）

Front-end

ASML · AMAT · Lam · TEL

Next inspection  AOI · metrology · SEM (sampled)

Transistors and metal layers are built, which sets the performance of the chip itself.

02

Wafer Test (KGD)

晶圓針測（KGD）

Inspection

Advantest · Teradyne · MPI · CHPT

Inspection  This step is the check: electrical test

Identifies known good die. The yield gate before stacking, and the cheapest point at which to discard a part.

Wafer Sort / Measurement

Wafer Sort / 量測

Inspection

KLA · Onto · Nova

Inspection  This step is the check: AOI · metrology

Binning and data capture, which decides which die is paired with which.

STAGE 2

Top-Die Preparation

上層晶片前處理，SoIC 與 CoWoS 共用

The trunk shared by both routes. A wafer starts around 775µm thick and must be thinned until the TSVs are exposed before a die can conduct vertically. Front-end tools already dominate this stage.
04

Temporary Bonding

暫時貼合

Packaging

EVG · SUSS MicroTec · TOK (Materials)

Next inspection  Metrology (adhesive thickness, alignment)

Mounted on a carrier. A thinned wafer is brittle enough to shatter without one.

05

Post-Bond Cure

貼合後固化

Support

C SUN · Koyo Thermo

Next inspection  Metrology (warpage)

Cures the adhesive so the wafer does not shift during grinding.

06a

Coarse & Fine Grinding

粗磨／細磨

Packaging

DISCO · Tokyo Seimitsu · Okamoto

Next inspection  Metrology (thickness, total thickness variation)

Mechanical grinding removes roughly 700µm. Fast, but it leaves sub-surface micro-cracks.

06b

CMP Polishing

CMP 拋光

Front-end

AMAT · Ebara · ACM Research · Kinik (Consumables)

Next inspection  AOI (scratches, slurry residue) · metrology · SEM (on excursion)

Chemical plus mechanical action removes only 1 to 5µm but eliminates grinding damage. Two different tools run back to back.

07

Wafer Vacuum Lamination

晶圓真空壓膜

Support

Takatori · Nitto Denko (Materials)

Next inspection  AOI (bubbles, wrinkles)

Lamination under vacuum, so no trapped bubbles turn into downstream defects.

08

TSV Reveal / RDL Patterning

TSV 背鑽／RDL 圖形化

Front-end

AMAT · Lam · TEL · Veeco

Next inspection  AOI (line defects) · metrology (linewidth, overlay) · X-ray (TSV fill) · SEM

Lithography, etch and deposition. Front-end fab tools, running inside a packaging flow.

09

Debonding

解鍵合

Packaging

EVG · SUSS MicroTec

Next inspection  AOI (breakage, adhesive residue)

Removes the temporary carrier by laser or mechanical release. A common source of breakage loss.

10

Dicing / Die Picking

切割／揀晶

Packaging

DISCO · Tokyo Seimitsu · ASMPT

Next inspection  AOI (edge chipping, cracks)

Singulated into individual die and sorted on the test result. The two routes diverge after this point.

The routes split here. The only difference is orientation: SoIC stacks die vertically, CoWoS lays them side by side on an interposer. They do not compete, and in practice they are frequently combined.

3A SoIC

System on Integrated Chips — vertical stacking

Direct copper-to-copper bonding, with no bumps and no solder. Surfaces must be flat to the nanometre and free of oxide, which is why half the tool set is front-end equipment.

3B CoWoS

Chip on Wafer on Substrate — lateral integration

Die sit side by side on an interposer, connected by micro-bumps and underfill. The problem being solved is the reticle limit: fitting more silicon into one package than a single exposure field allows.

3B-C Co-Packaged Optics Optional branch — not on the mandatory path
Who takes this branch

Switch silicon: Broadcom Tomahawk, NVIDIA Quantum-X and Spectrum-X, Marvell. Their job is moving data out of the package.

Who does not

GPUs. Their bandwidth bottleneck is the HBM inside the package rather than external I/O, so there is no reason to take on the cost and risk of optics.

TSMC COUPE stacks all three technologies at once
1SoICHybrid-bonds the 65nm electronic IC to the photonic IC to form the optical engine
2CoWoSPlaces the optical engine and the customer ASIC on one interposer
3CPOThe result: optics move inside the package, replacing the pluggable module outside it

The only product using all three at once. COUPE on Substrate entered production in the second half of 2026, with the CoWoS-integrated version to follow. The SoIC here sits inside the optical engine and is not used to stack compute die.

Packaging

Coherent · Lumentum · Broadcom ｜ LandMark

Next inspection  Optical power · wavelength

Laser diode attach, or an external light source instead. The laser is acutely temperature-sensitive and sits beside an ASIC dissipating hundreds of watts.

Packaging

ficonTEC · ASMPT · K&S · Shinkawa

Next inspection  Active alignment metrology (coupling efficiency measured live)

Aligns the fiber to the waveguide entrance at sub-micron precision. One micron off and insertion loss rises sharply.

Packaging

Next inspection  AOI (adhesive line) · pull test (sampled)

C5

Optical Test

光學測試

Inspection

Keysight · EXFO · Chroma

Inspection  This step is the check: insertion loss, coupling efficiency, bit error rate

The electrical yield is hard enough on its own; optical yield now multiplies on top of it. This is the core reason CPO yield is difficult.

Inspection

33

Final Inspection / Outgoing

最終檢查／出貨

Inspection

KLA · Camtek · YXLON (X-ray)

Inspection  This step is the check: AOI · metrology · X-ray

Visual and dimensional check, then ship.

In practice the two are combined. The diagram shows SoIC and CoWoS as parallel routes, but a real product usually runs SoIC first to bond logic die vertically, then feeds that stacked module into CoWoS at 3B-1 to sit on the interposer alongside the HBM. TSMC markets the combination as 3DFabric. Read the map as which technology applies to which layer, not as a choice between two options.
STAGE 4

Board-Level Assembly & System Integration

板級組裝與系統整合

Both routes converge here. This is board-level work, and the equipment is one to two orders of magnitude cheaper than anything above. Since AI servers moved to liquid cooling, however, cold plate assembly and leak testing in group G have become a genuine production bottleneck, and the main source of Taiwanese value in this stage.

Inspection  This step is the check: dimensional metrology · visual AOI · moisture sensitivity level

Boards, passives, connectors and packaged parts are inspected before stocking. Packaged parts are extremely expensive, so lot codes and moisture levels are locked down here.

B Surface Mount, First Side 表面貼裝 SMT（正面）
36

Solder Paste Printing

Packaging

ASMPT (DEK)· Panasonic · Yamaha · Ekra ｜ Stencils: Photo Stencil · Koh Young

Next inspection  SPI 3D solder paste metrology

A stencil prints solder paste onto the pads. More than half of all board-level solder defects originate here, so this step governs everything downstream.

37

Solder Paste Inspection

SPI 錫膏檢測

Inspection

Koh Young · CyberOptics · Parmi · TRI

Inspection  This step is the check: 3D paste metrology, 100%

Measures paste volume, area and offset on every pad. A bad board is wiped and reprinted before placement, at almost no cost.

38

SMT Placement

SMT 貼片

Packaging

ASMPT · Fuji · Panasonic · Yamaha ｜ Contract mfg: Foxconn · Quanta · Wistron

Next inspection  Post-reflow AOI · X-ray (BGA joints)

Packages and passives are placed on the board. OAM and SXM accelerator cards take shape here.

39

Reflow Soldering

迴流焊

Packaging

Rehm · BTU · Heller · Kurtz Ersa

Next inspection  AOI (step 40) · X-ray (step 41) ｜ Process monitor: reflow profiling

A multi-zone nitrogen oven melts the paste into joints. The thermal profile has to be retuned for board thickness and mass, and large boards are hard to keep even.

40

Post-Reflow AOI

迴流後 AOI

Inspection

Koh Young · Omron · Saki · TRI

Inspection  This step is the check: 100% optical inspection

Checks open joints, offset, tombstoning and bridging. AOI handles the joints it can see; the next step handles the ones it cannot.

41

Automated X-ray Inspection

X-ray 檢測（AXI）

Inspection

Nordson · YXLON · ViTrox · TRI · Saki

Inspection  This step is the check: penetrating inspection, 100% of BGAs

The balls under a BGA or a large package are hidden by the component itself, so only X-ray can find voids, offset and cold joints.
