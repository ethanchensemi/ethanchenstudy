Component Terminology

TermDefinitionSuppliers
Light Source
InP SubstrateThe indium phosphide wafer the laser is built onSumitomo Electric · JX Advanced Metals · AXT
EpitaxyThin light-emitting layers grown on the InP waferIQE · VPEC · LandMark · IntelliEPI | Tools: Aixtron · Veeco · Riber
Laser DieThe laser chip cut from that wafer. It gives off steady lightLumentum · Coherent · Broadcom · Mitsubishi Electric · Sumitomo Electric · LandMark · LuxNet
Chip on SubmountThe laser die mounted on a carrier that spreads its heatELASER · LuxNet · Truelight
ELSExternal laser source. The laser module at the front panelLumentum · Coherent · TFC · ELASER
PM Fibre PigtailThe fibre that carries laser light from the ELS to each engineCorning · Fujikura · YOFC
Optical Engine
SOI WaferThe silicon wafer the PIC is made on: waveguide layer, buried oxide and silicon handleSoitec · Shin-Etsu · SUMCO
ModulatorPuts data onto the lightBuilt in the PIC: TSMC · Tower · GlobalFoundries · UMC
PhotodetectorTurns incoming light back into an electrical signalBuilt in the PIC: TSMC · Tower · GlobalFoundries · UMC
PICThe photonic chip. Carries the light and holds the modulators and photodetectorsTSMC · Tower · GlobalFoundries · UMC
EICThe electronic chip. Drives the modulators and reads the photodetectorsTSMC · GlobalFoundries
Hybrid BondThe copper-to-copper joint between EIC and PIC, with no bumpsTSMC | Tools: Besi · EVG · SUSS MicroTec · ASMPT
Optical EngineEIC and PIC joined together. Converts between electrical and opticalTSMC · SPIL | Design: NVIDIA · Broadcom · Marvell
Fibre
Grating CouplerThe spot on the PIC where light enters and leavesBuilt in the PIC: TSMC · Tower · GlobalFoundries · UMC
FAUFibre array unit. Holds the fibres in line with the couplerTFC · FOCI · Browave · EZconn · Senko · Corning · Coherent
Fibre ShuffleRibbon fibre from each FAU to the front panelEZconn · Browave · JPC · BizLink
MPOThe fibre connector on the front panelUS Conec · Senko · Corning
