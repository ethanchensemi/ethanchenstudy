3.5

Advertising

How each ad impression is sold at auction and the AI systems that run Meta’s advertising.

Auction Mechanics

StepDescription
OpportunityA user opens Feed, Stories, Reels, Status or Threads, creating a slot for an ad
RetrievalAndromeda narrows tens of millions of eligible ads to a few thousand candidates
PredictionRanking models estimate how likely the user is to click, buy or install after seeing each ad
Total ValueEach ad scores its bid multiplied by the estimated action rate, plus an ad-quality score, and the highest total value wins
ChargeMost ads are charged per impression, so better predictions deliver more results per impression and support higher prices
FeedbackConversion data from the Meta Pixel and Conversions API shows what each ad returned and trains the models that predict the next result

AI Systems

SystemDescriptionRevenue Impact
AndromedaThe retrieval system that picks candidate ads, now running on NVIDIA, AMD and MTIA chipsAds quality up 14% on Facebook after retrieval and early ranking were merged, 3Q25
GEMMeta's largest ads model, which trains the smaller ranking models used at serving timeFacebook ad clicks up 8.3% and conversions up 15.7% from improved user-understanding models with GEM and sequence learning, 2Q26
LatticeOne large model architecture that replaces many single-objective ranking modelsAds quality up 12%, 4Q25
Generative RecommenderLanguage models that reason over ad content and user preferences, first used in ads retrieval in 2Q26Gains described as notable but not yet quantified
Advantage+Automated campaigns in which AI sets audience, placement, creative and budgetUS$75bn annual run-rate, 2Q26
Creative ToolsAI-generated ad images, video and text, with Muse Image announced for Advantage+ creative in 3Q26Used by 9m small businesses, 2Q26
