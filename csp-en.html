Cloud Service Providers — Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta

Cloud service providers (CSPs) are the largest buyers of AI servers, accelerators, networking and data-centre capacity, so their capital-expenditure plans set demand across most of the supply chain covered on this site. This page takes the four US hyperscalers one at a time, each in six parts: latest quarter, capex and capacity, custom silicon, AI monetisation, Taiwan supply chain and broker views. Figures are drawn from broker reports published between April and September 2026; Microsoft periods follow its June fiscal year.
