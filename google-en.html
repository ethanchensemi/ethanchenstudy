Google Search

How Search ads are sold at auction, and how AI features affect Search revenue.

Auction Mechanics

StepDescription
QueryA user searches, and Google judges whether the query has commercial intent
EligibilityAdvertisers whose keywords, location and audience settings match enter the auction
RankingAds are ranked by Ad Rank, which combines the bid, predicted quality (expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience), the search context and other factors
PriceThe winner pays per click, usually less than its maximum bid, so quality lowers the price of a click
FeedbackConversion tracking shows the advertiser what each click returned, which sets the next bid

AI Features

FeatureDescriptionRevenue Impact
AI OverviewsWrites a summary above the results for many queries, with ads shown within and around itFewer clicks on some informational queries, offset by more searches overall
AI ModeA conversational search mode for longer questions and follow-ups, which Google has brought together with AI Overviews into one Search experienceNew query volume and new ad placements inside answers
AI in Ad BuyingGemini-based tools write the ads, pick the queries and set the bidsBetter conversion lets advertisers pay more per click
Chat AssistantsGemini, ChatGPT and others answer questions directlySome commercial queries may move to assistants, where ad formats are still young

4.2

TPU v10

How TPU v10 changes the way the chip is designed, packaged and split among suppliers.

What Changes with v10

ChangeTodayv10What It Means
Design PartnersOne Lead Partner per ChipMediaTek plus Block SuppliersBroadcom is the lead design partner on v7 and v8t, and MediaTek on v8i and v9. On v10 MediaTek stays the lead integrator, while separate blocks are open to Marvell, GUC and other design firms.
Business ModelTurnkeyCustomer-Owned ToolingUnder turnkey, the design partner buys the compute die from the foundry and sells Google a finished chip. On v10 Google designs the die, owns its photomask (the stencil set that prints it) and has it made, then hands MediaTek a known good die, one tested before packaging. The compute die no longer passes through the design partner’s revenue.
Packaging2.5D3.5D2.5D places dies side by side on a silicon interposer or bridge. 3.5D adds a 3D step, stacking the compute die on other dies, which needs a 3D IC design partner.
Package Size9 Reticles>12 ReticlesA reticle is the largest area one lithography exposure prints: 26 × 33mm, or 858mm². More than 12 reticles is over 10,296mm² of silicon in one package, room for more compute and memory per TPU. At this size v10 stays on Intel EMIB-T, which links neighbouring dies through small silicon bridges set in the substrate.

TPU v10 Work Split

BlockSupplierWhat It DoesChange from Today
Compute DieGoogleRuns the AI calculationsMoves from the design partner to Google, which owns the photomask and delivers a known good die; back-end layout design is open to other design firms
HBMSK hynix, Samsung, MicronStacked memory placed beside the compute dieUnchanged
Electrical I/O DieMediaTekSends data to other chips over copper, through SerDes circuits that turn parallel data into a fast serial streamSame role as on v8i and v9, with MediaTek’s own SerDes IP and a larger die than on v9
Memory I/OMarvell or OthersLinks the compute die to memoryA separate block open to firms other than MediaTek
3D IC and Co-Packaged OpticsGUC or OthersStacks the compute die on the dies beneath it and builds optical links into the package in place of copperNew with 3.5D, and open to firms other than MediaTek
Package IntegrationMediaTek, on Intel EMIB-TAssembles the compute die, I/O die and HBM into the finished TPUSame role as on v9
