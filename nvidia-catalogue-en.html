1.5

Networking Silicon

Silicon

Host is the server side of the link: the CPU and its memory on the compute tray. Fabric is the network beyond it: the switches and cabling that carry traffic between trays, racks and sites. Quantum runs InfiniBand, a purpose-built lossless fabric whose switches, adapters and cables NVIDIA supplies end to end, while Spectrum runs Ethernet, the standard data centres already operate, adapted for AI traffic in hardware rather than replaced.

Protocol

An interconnect is an ability built into the silicon, not a box. Every GPU has to carry NVLink on its own die before an NVLink Switch has anything to route: the switch connects GPUs that can already speak it, it does not give them the ability.
